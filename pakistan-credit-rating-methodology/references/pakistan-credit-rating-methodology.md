# Pakistan Credit Rating Methodology Reference

## Source

- SECP, Credit Rating Companies Regulations, 2016 (issued under the Securities Act, 2015): `https://www.secp.gov.pk`
- PACRA (The Pakistan Credit Rating Agency Limited), rating scale and methodology documents: `https://www.pacra.com`
- VIS Credit Rating Company Limited (formerly JCR-VIS), rating scale and methodology documents: `https://www.vis.com.pk`
- IOSCO, Code of Conduct Fundamentals for Credit Rating Agencies (reference framework SECP's 2016 Regulations align with): `https://www.iosco.org`

## Regulatory Structure

- SECP licenses and regulates Credit Rating Companies (CRCs) under the Credit Rating Companies Regulations, 2016, issued under the Securities Act, 2015. The regulations cover licensing eligibility, ongoing obligations (accounting/financial-record keeping, disclosure), restrictions on conflicts of interest, and grounds for suspension/cancellation of a CRC's license.
- Two SECP-licensed CRCs currently operate in Pakistan's domestic market: PACRA (established 1994, the country's first credit rating agency) and VIS Credit Rating Company Ltd. (formerly JCR-VIS Credit Rating Company).

## Rating Scale Structure

- **Long-term entity/instrument scale**: runs from AAA (highest) down through D (default), with a generally understood investment-grade/speculative-grade cutoff partway down the scale. Exact notch definitions (e.g., what distinguishes "AA" from "AA-") are set out in each agency's own published rating-scale document and should be sourced there rather than assumed identical across agencies.
- **Short-term scale**: a separate, shorter scale (e.g., A-1, A-2, A-3, ... down to C/D-equivalent short-term categories) used for obligations with shorter maturities.
- **Specialized scales**: fund stability ratings, REIT ratings, and other structured-product ratings use distinct scales, denoted with suffixes (e.g., "(f)" for fund ratings) to avoid confusion with standard issuer/instrument ratings.

## Methodology Framework

Both PACRA and VIS structure entity ratings around two pillars, synthesized into a single forward-looking relative-default-likelihood opinion:

1. **Business Risk**: industry dynamics, competitive position, management quality and governance.
2. **Financial Risk**: capital structure, coverage ratios, liquidity position, profitability trends.

## Rating Actions and Timelines

- **Initial rating**: assigned following the agency's review process at issuer/instrument request (or, for some regulatory purposes, mandated).
- **Surveillance**: ratings are reviewed on an ongoing basis, typically at least semi-annually, and can be revised (upgraded/downgraded) between scheduled reviews if material new information emerges.
- **Rating outlook**: a directional view (positive/stable/negative) over a multi-year horizon, signaling the likely direction of a future rating action without specifying timing.
- **Rating watch**: a shorter-horizon flag tied to a specific pending event (e.g., a merger, regulatory action, or funding event) that could trigger an imminent rating change.

## Issuer vs. Instrument Ratings

An instrument (e.g., a specific TFC or Sukuk issuance) can carry a rating that differs from its issuer's own entity rating, driven by instrument-specific features such as seniority, security/collateral, or structural credit enhancement (e.g., guarantees, over-collateralization).

## Extraction Notes

- Always confirm which agency (PACRA or VIS) is the source before citing a specific scale notch or suffix convention, since the two agencies' scales are similar in spirit but not necessarily identical in every technical respect.
- Never state a specific company's or instrument's current rating as a static fact — ratings change via ongoing surveillance, and a cited rating must be sourced to a current, dated rating action from the agency's own published report.
- Distinguish rating outlook (multi-year directional signal) from rating watch (short-horizon, event-driven flag) — these are commonly confused in casual usage but carry different meanings in agency methodology.
