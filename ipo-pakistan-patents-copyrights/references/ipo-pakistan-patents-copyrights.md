## Institutional Structure

IPO Pakistan (established under the IPO Pakistan Act 2012) is the federal umbrella body overseeing three constituent registries/offices, each governed by its own substantive statute:

| Right | Office | Governing Statute |
|---|---|---|
| Patents | Patent Office (Karachi) | Patents Ordinance, 2000 |
| Copyright | Copyright Office | Copyright Ordinance, 1962 (as amended) |
| Trademarks | Trademarks Registry | Trade Marks Ordinance regime (see `ipo-pakistan-trademarks`) |

## Patents Ordinance, 2000 — Key Mechanics

- **Patentability**: an invention must be new, involve an inventive step, and be capable of industrial application. Non-patentable subject matter includes discoveries, scientific theories, mathematical methods, and other categories listed in the Ordinance (e.g., certain methods of medical treatment/diagnosis).
- **Filing**: applicants may file a provisional specification followed by a complete specification within the prescribed period, or file a complete specification directly.
- **Examination**: not automatic — substantive examination (including a prior-art search) occurs only after a formal request for examination is filed within the prescribed period; an unexamined application can otherwise remain pending/deemed abandoned.
- **Publication and opposition**: accepted applications are published, opening a window for third-party opposition before formal grant.
- **Term**: 20 years from the filing date, contingent on payment of periodic renewal/maintenance fees (verify current fee schedule with IPO Pakistan, as fees are revised periodically).
- **Priority**: Pakistan's Paris Convention membership allows convention-priority claims from an earlier foreign filing within the applicable priority period (conventionally 12 months for patents).

## Copyright Ordinance, 1962 (as amended) — Key Mechanics

- **Automatic protection**: copyright subsists automatically upon creation/fixation of an original literary, dramatic, musical, or artistic work, or upon production of a cinematograph film or sound recording — no registration is required to create the right, consistent with Berne Convention principles.
- **Registration**: optional, administered by the Copyright Office; creates a public record and an evidentiary presumption of ownership/authorship useful in later infringement disputes, but registration is not a precondition to the underlying right.
- **Software**: computer programs are protected as literary works under Pakistani copyright law.
- **Term**: general rule for literary/dramatic/musical/artistic works is the life of the author plus a fixed post-mortem period (commonly cited as 50 years, aligned with the Berne Convention minimum — verify the precise figure in the current consolidated Ordinance, since Pakistan's term could differ from the Berne floor). Cinematograph films, sound recordings, photographs, and government/anonymous works are typically calculated from the year of publication rather than the author's life, and the applicable period should be confirmed against the current Ordinance text for the specific category.

## Infringement and Enforcement

- Both statutes provide civil remedies — injunctions, damages or accounts of profits, and delivery-up/destruction of infringing material — and criminal penalties for infringement.
- Enforcement intersects with customs border-measures against counterfeit and pirated goods at import/export stage.
- Specialized IP tribunals exist in some provinces for faster adjudication of IP disputes, supplementing ordinary civil court jurisdiction.

## Extraction Notes

- Never state that copyright requires registration to exist in Pakistan — it is a Berne-Convention-consistent automatic right; registration is optional evidentiary support only.
- Keep patent term (20 years from filing) clearly separate from copyright term (life-plus-years, category-dependent) — do not apply one term rule to both regimes.
- Flag exact current fees, renewal deadlines, or any pending amendment to either Ordinance as needing verification against IPO Pakistan's current schedule or the Gazette, rather than asserting a fixed figure from memory.
- Keep this skill's scope (patents and copyright) distinct from `ipo-pakistan-trademarks` (trademarks), which is a separate registry, separate statute, and separate process even though both sit under the IPO Pakistan umbrella.
