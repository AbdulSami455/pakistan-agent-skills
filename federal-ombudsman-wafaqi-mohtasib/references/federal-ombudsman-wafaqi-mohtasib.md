## Founding Instrument and Institutional Design

The Office of the Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman) was established under the Establishment of the Office of Wafaqi Mohtasib (Ombudsman) Order, 1983, a Presidential Order that created an independent institution to investigate citizen complaints of maladministration against federal government agencies, ministries, divisions, attached departments, and federal statutory bodies/corporations. The Mohtasib is appointed by the President of Pakistan for a fixed term and functions independently in adjudicating individual complaints once appointed.

## Maladministration — Core Concept

The Order's concept of maladministration is intentionally broad, generally encompassing:

- decisions, processes, or omissions contrary to law, rules, or regulations
- decisions that are perverse, arbitrary, or unreasonable, or based on irrelevant grounds
- conduct involving corruption, nepotism, or abuse/excess of administrative authority
- unreasonable delay in performing a statutory duty or function

This framework is designed to capture a wide range of administrative failure rather than a narrow closed list of triggering acts.

## Jurisdiction and Exclusions

- Jurisdiction: federal government agencies, ministries, divisions, attached departments, and federal statutory corporations/bodies.
- Commonly cited exclusions: matters sub judice before a court or tribunal; certain personnel/service matters of civil servants (subject to specific statutory carve-outs and evolving case law); matters concerning foreign affairs, national defence, and the armed forces.
- The precise, current exclusion list and any amendments should be verified against the consolidated text of the 1983 Order, since exclusions have been subject to legislative and judicial refinement over time.
- The Wafaqi Mohtasib's jurisdiction is federal only; provincial-government maladministration falls outside its remit, addressed instead (where established) by provincial ombudsman institutions.

## Complaint Process

- Complaints are typically filed free of cost with the Wafaqi Mohtasib Secretariat (Islamabad) or its regional offices, without a requirement for legal representation.
- The Mohtasib has investigative powers, including summoning records and officials.
- Outcomes can include a recommendation or decision directing corrective administrative action, implementation of a remedy, or in practice, compensation — informality and accessibility relative to court litigation is a defining design feature.

## Appeal to the President

A party aggrieved by a Wafaqi Mohtasib decision may file a representation to the President of Pakistan within the prescribed period. The President functions as the appellate authority over Mohtasib findings — a structurally distinct appeal route from the ordinary judicial appellate hierarchy.

## Specialized Ombudsman Institutions in Pakistan

| Institution | Sector | Enabling Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman) | General federal-agency maladministration | Establishment of the Office of Wafaqi Mohtasib (Ombudsman) Order, 1983 |
| Federal Tax Ombudsman | Tax administration (FBR) maladministration | Federal Tax Ombudsman Ordinance, 2000 |
| Banking Mohtasib Pakistan | Banking-sector consumer complaints | Established under SBP/Banking Companies Ordinance-linked framework |
| Insurance Ombudsman / Federal Insurance Ombudsman | Insurance-sector complaints | Insurance Ordinance-linked framework |

Provincial ombudsman (Mohtasib) offices also exist in some provinces for provincial-government maladministration, operating separately from the federal institution.

## Extraction Notes

- Do not conflate the Wafaqi Mohtasib's administrative-grievance process with a criminal proceeding; NAB's accountability pipeline is a separate track entirely.
- Always name the correct specialized ombudsman (Federal Tax Ombudsman, Banking Mohtasib, Insurance/Federal Insurance Ombudsman) when the complaint is sector-specific, rather than defaulting to the general Wafaqi Mohtasib.
- The appellate authority over a Wafaqi Mohtasib decision is the President (via representation), not a court.
- Flag current filing timelines, the exact appeal window, and the current jurisdictional-exclusion list as needing verification against the consolidated 1983 Order text.
