## FPSC — Statutory Basis and Role

Article 242 of the Constitution provides for the establishment of public service commissions at the federal and provincial levels. FPSC operates under the Federal Public Service Commission Ordinance, 1977, as the body responsible for recruitment to federal civil-service posts through competitive examination and other prescribed processes. Beyond the annual CSS examination, FPSC conducts numerous other federal recruitment tests for posts advertised outside the CSS cadre (various BPS grades across ministries, divisions, and attached departments).

## CSS Examination — Structure

- **Written stage**: comprises compulsory papers (commonly including English Essay, English Precis and Composition, General Science & Ability, Current Affairs, Pakistan Affairs, and Islamic Studies/Comparative Religions for non-Muslim candidates) and optional papers chosen by the candidate from an FPSC-approved list of subject groups (e.g., business/accounting-related subjects, governance/political-science/international-relations subjects, history subjects, literature/language subjects, science/mathematics subjects, law subjects). Candidates select a fixed number of optional papers subject to FPSC's rules on permissible subject combinations.
- **Qualifying threshold**: candidates must meet a minimum aggregate/per-paper qualifying mark in the written stage to proceed.
- **Psychological assessment**: a psychological/psychometric evaluation stage follows the written qualifying stage.
- **Viva voce (interview)**: a final interview stage precedes preparation of the merit list.
- **Merit list and allocation**: successful candidates are ranked by combined merit and allocated to federal occupational groups based on rank and stated preference.

Exact current paper titles, marks distribution, minimum qualifying marks, and total exam marks should be verified against the current FPSC CSS syllabus/advertisement for the relevant examination year, as the structure has been revised more than once, including a notable restructuring exercise in recent years.

## Federal Occupational Groups (Commonly Cited)

- Pakistan Administration Service (PAS)
- Police Service of Pakistan (PSP)
- Foreign Service of Pakistan (FSP)
- Pakistan Customs Service
- Inland Revenue Service (IRS)
- Office Management Group (OMG)
- Other specialized groups historically including Postal Group, Railways (Commercial & Transportation), Information Group, Commerce & Trade Group, and Secretariat Group

Exact current group names, count, and cadre-composition rules should be checked against the current Establishment Division notification / Civil Servants (Composition, Cadre and Seniority) rules in force, since group structure and naming have been periodically revised.

## Eligibility (General Structure)

- Minimum age: commonly 21 years at the time of application.
- Maximum age: commonly around 30 years, with standard relaxation for in-service government servants and additional relaxations for other prescribed categories.
- Minimum educational qualification: typically at least a bachelor's degree, with group-specific additional qualification requirements for certain technical groups.

Exact current age limits, relaxation categories, and qualification detail should be verified against the current year's official FPSC CSS advertisement/rules, since these are set by rules subject to periodic revision.

## Provincial Equivalents

| Commission | Province | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| PPSC | Punjab | Provincial civil-service recruitment |
| SPSC | Sindh | Provincial civil-service recruitment |
| KPPSC | Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | Provincial civil-service recruitment |
| BPSC | Balochistan | Provincial civil-service recruitment |

Each operates under its own provincial legislation, examination structure, and cadre system, legally and institutionally separate from FPSC. A CSS pass does not confer eligibility for a provincial civil-service post, and success in a provincial exam does not confer eligibility for a federal CSS-cadre post.

## Extraction Notes

- Keep FPSC/CSS (federal) clearly separate from PPSC/SPSC/KPPSC/BPSC (provincial) — different legislation, different cadres, non-interchangeable outcomes.
- FPSC administers the CSS exam directly; NTS is a separate, broader-mandate testing organization not responsible for CSS administration (see `nts-testing-service` for NTS's own scope).
- Describe CSS group allocation as merit-and-preference based following the full multi-stage process, not a direct upfront application to one group.
- Flag exact current age limits, marks distribution, and paper lists as needing verification against the current official FPSC CSS syllabus/advertisement.
